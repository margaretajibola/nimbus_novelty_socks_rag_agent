# Expenses and Travel Policy

Last updated: February 2026

## 1. General Rules

Spend company money like it is your own. Keep every receipt. Submit expense claims in Receipto within 30 days of the expense. Claims submitted after 30 days may be declined.

## 2. Approvals

- Up to $500 CAD: your direct manager approves
- $500 to $2,500 CAD: your manager and your department director approve
- Over $2,500 CAD: the VP of your department must also approve

Always get approval before the spend, not after.

## 3. Travel

Book travel through the company travel page on Leaflet. Flights should be economy class. For flights longer than 6 hours, you may book premium economy.

- Hotel: up to $220 CAD per night
- Meals while travelling: up to $75 CAD per day in total, including tips
- Ground transport: use public transit or a taxi or rideshare where needed. Car rentals need manager approval.
- Personal vehicle: reimbursed at $0.70 CAD per kilometre

## 4. What We Do Not Reimburse

- Alcohol
- Minibar items and hotel movies
- Traffic or parking fines
- Personal travel extensions, unless you pay the difference

## 5. Client Entertainment

Client meals and entertainment are limited to $100 CAD per person and need director approval in advance. List all attendees on the claim.

## 6. Home Office Stipend

Remote and hybrid employees get a one-time home office stipend of $600 CAD for a desk, chair or similar equipment. Nimbus also pays $50 CAD per month toward home internet. Submit the one-time stipend as an expense in Receipto. The monthly internet payment is added to payroll automatically after you submit a first internet bill.

## 7. Learning Budget Claims

Learning budget claims (see the Employee Handbook) follow the same process. Attach the receipt and write "Learning budget" in the category field. Anything over $500 CAD still needs manager approval before you pay.

## 8. Reimbursement Timing

Approved claims are paid in the next regular payroll after approval.
