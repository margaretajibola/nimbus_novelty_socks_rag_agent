# Employee FAQ

Last updated: March 2026

## When do we get paid?

Payroll runs every second Friday (bi-weekly) by direct deposit. If a payday falls on a public holiday, you are paid the business day before.

## Who do I contact about payroll problems?

Email payroll@nimbussocks.example. Include your employee ID and the pay date in question.

## Who handles HR questions?

Marcus Teo leads People and HR. Email people@nimbussocks.example or message the #ask-hr channel on Slack. Questions about leave or benefits are usually answered within 1 business day.

## Where is the office and what are the hours?

The office is at 214 Harbour Street, Halifax. It is open from 8:00 to 18:00 on weekdays. Outside those hours you need your key fob to enter.

## How do I book a meeting room?

Use the Roomly app. Rooms can be booked up to 14 days ahead. Release the room if your meeting ends early.

## Is there parking?

There are 10 parking spots behind the building, first come first served. There is also secure bike storage and a shower on the ground floor.

## Can I bring my dog to the office?

Dogs are allowed on Wednesdays only. Register your dog with the office manager first, and make sure it is vaccinated and friendly. If a coworker is allergic, the dog stays home.

## How do I claim my free socks?

Go to the Nimbus staff store page and choose your pairs. You can claim up to 12 pairs per calendar year. They ship free to your home or can be picked up at the office.

## How do I get a new laptop or equipment?

Open an IT ticket. Standard laptops are replaced every 3 years. For a broken device, tell IT as soon as possible so they can arrange a replacement.

## What is the process for a promotion?

Promotions are discussed during the June and December reviews. Your manager submits a promotion case to the department director. HR confirms the decision, and changes start at the beginning of the next quarter.

## Who do I contact if I see something that breaks our values or the law?

Email conduct@nimbussocks.example. Reports can be anonymous. Nimbus does not allow retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

## What should I do on my first day?

Arrive at the office at 9:30. You will meet Marcus Teo, get your laptop from Sofia Alvarez's team, and have lunch with your team on the company.
