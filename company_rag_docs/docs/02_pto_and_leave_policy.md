# Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

Last updated: January 2026

## 1. Annual PTO

Paid time off depends on how long you have worked at Nimbus:

- Years 0 to 3 (less than 3 full years of service): 20 days per year
- Years 3 to 7 (3 or more full years, less than 7): 25 days per year
- 7 or more full years of service: 30 days per year

PTO accrues monthly. New employees can use accrued PTO after finishing probation.

## 2. Carryover

You can carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 or they are lost. PTO is not paid out in cash except where required by law when employment ends.

## 3. Requesting Time Off

Request PTO in Leaflet, our HR system. For absences of 3 or more consecutive days, submit the request at least 2 weeks in advance. Managers should approve or decline within 3 business days.

## 4. Public Holidays and Winter Break

Nimbus observes all statutory holidays in Nova Scotia. In addition, the office is closed from December 24 to January 1 for the winter break. Winter break days do not count against your PTO.

## 5. Sick Days

Each employee gets 8 paid sick days per year. You do not need a doctor's note for up to 3 consecutive sick days. For 4 or more consecutive days, a note is required. Tell your manager as early as you can on the day you are sick.

## 6. Parental Leave

Nimbus tops up government parental benefits to 80 percent of your regular salary for a limited time:

- Birth parent: 16 weeks of top-up
- Non-birth parent or adoptive parent: 8 weeks of top-up

You need to have completed probation to qualify for the top-up. Tell HR at least 8 weeks before your expected leave start date where possible.

## 7. Bereavement Leave

You get 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, partner, child, parent, sibling). You get 2 paid days for the death of an extended family member (such as a grandparent, aunt or uncle).

## 8. Other Paid Days

- Birthday day: 1 paid day off, to be taken in the month of your birthday
- Volunteer days: 2 paid days per year to volunteer for a registered charity
