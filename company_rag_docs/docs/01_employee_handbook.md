# Nimbus Novelty Socks, Inc. Employee Handbook

Last updated: January 2026

## 1. About Nimbus

Nimbus Novelty Socks, Inc. was founded in 2016 and designs and sells novelty socks online and through 40 retail partners across Canada. We have about 85 employees. Our headquarters is at 214 Harbour Street, Halifax, Nova Scotia. Our CEO is Dana Whitfield, our head of People and HR is Marcus Teo, and our IT and security lead is Sofia Alvarez.

## 2. Our Values

We have four values: be kind, ship small, stay curious, and keep it fun. Managers are expected to use these values when giving feedback and making hiring decisions.

## 3. Working Hours

The standard work week is 37.5 hours. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Eastern Time. During core hours you should be reachable on Slack and available for meetings. Outside core hours you can set your own schedule, as long as you agree it with your manager.

## 4. Hybrid Work

Nimbus is a hybrid company. Employees in the Halifax area work from the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. The other days can be worked from home.

You may work remotely from another Canadian province for up to 4 weeks per calendar year. You may work from outside Canada for up to 2 weeks per calendar year. Working abroad needs your manager's approval and at least 30 days' notice to HR, because of tax and legal rules.

## 5. Communication

Slack is for daily communication. Email is for external contacts and formal announcements. You are expected to reply to messages within 4 business hours. If you are in focus time, set your Slack status so people know.

## 6. Dress Code

Dress is casual. The one rule is Sock Friday: on Fridays, everyone in the office wears their most ridiculous socks. Customer-facing staff should wear a clean Nimbus shirt at trade shows.

## 7. Probation and Reviews

New employees have a 3-month probation period. Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Salary changes, if any, take effect at the start of the next quarter after the December review.

## 8. Learning Budget

Every employee gets a learning budget of $1,200 CAD per calendar year for courses, books, conferences and certifications. Unused budget does not roll over. Submit learning expenses using the process in the Expenses and Travel Policy.

## 9. Employee Discount and Free Socks

Employees get a 50 percent discount on everything in the Nimbus online store. Each employee also gets 12 free pairs of socks per year, which you can claim through the Nimbus staff store page.

## 10. Referral Bonus

If you refer someone who is hired and stays for at least 6 months, you receive a $1,500 CAD referral bonus, paid with the next payroll after the 6-month mark.
