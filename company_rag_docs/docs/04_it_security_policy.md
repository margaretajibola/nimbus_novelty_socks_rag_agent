# IT and Security Policy

Last updated: March 2026

## 1. Passwords and Access

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Use the company password manager for all work accounts. Do not reuse passwords between accounts. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for email, Slack, Leaflet, Receipto and the production systems.

## 2. Company Laptops

All company laptops are encrypted and managed by IT. Laptops are replaced every 3 years. Do not disable the disk encryption or the automatic updates. Do not lend your laptop to anyone, including family members.

## 3. Lost or Stolen Devices

If a laptop or phone with company data is lost or stolen, report it to security@nimbussocks.example within 1 hour of noticing. IT will lock and wipe the device remotely. Reporting quickly will not get you in trouble. Waiting will.

## 4. Installing Software

You must request new software through an IT ticket. IT reviews each request and normally responds within 2 business days. Do not install software from unknown sources.

## 5. Public Wi-Fi

Use the company VPN whenever you connect from public Wi-Fi, such as cafes, airports and hotels.

## 6. Phishing

If you receive a suspicious email, click the Report Phish button in your email client. Do not forward it to coworkers. If you clicked a link or entered a password, tell IT right away so your accounts can be secured.

## 7. Data Classification

Data is split into four levels: public, internal, confidential and restricted.

- Public: marketing material and the website
- Internal: general company documents and plans
- Confidential: customer lists, supplier contracts and financial reports
- Restricted: employee records and anything covered by privacy law

Restricted data may only be shared with people who need it for their job. Never email restricted data to a personal account.

## 8. Customer Payment Data

Nimbus never stores credit card numbers. All card payments go through Stripe. If a customer sends you card details in an email or chat, do not copy them anywhere. Delete the message and tell the customer to use the checkout page.

## 9. Security Incidents

The security team triages every reported incident within 2 hours during business hours. If an incident might involve customer data, the CEO and legal counsel are told the same day.

## 10. Leaving the Company

Access to all systems is revoked on the employee's last day. Laptops and equipment must be returned within 5 business days.
